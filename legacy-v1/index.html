      ❓ How to Play

      🟩 Daily Word Guess

      Guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries:

      • Green: Correct letter in the correct position.
      • Yellow: Correct letter in the wrong position.
      • Gray: Letter not in the word.

      🔢 Daily Sudoku

      Fill the 9×9 grid so every row, column, and 3×3 box contains digits 1 to 9 without repetition.

      Toggle ✏️ Notes to jot down pencil candidates. 3 errors allowed per day!

      💣 Daily Minesweeper

      Clear all safe squares on a 9×9 grid containing 10 hidden mines.

      • Numbers reveal how many mines touch that square.
      • Right-click or toggle Flag mode to place 🚩 flags.

      🟨 Daily Connections

      Group the 16 words into 4 secret categories of 4. Select 4 words and click Submit. You have 4 mistakes before reveal!

      📰 Daily Mini Crossword

      Fill the 5×5 grid with words based on the Across and Down clues. Tap squares to switch direction.

      🖼️ Daily Nonogram

      Use row and column number clues to fill in black tiles and reveal a hidden picture! Mark empty tiles with ❌.

      🧮 Daily 2048

      Use arrow keys, WASD, or swipe to slide matching tiles together. Combine identical numbers to reach 2048!

      🔍 Daily Word Search

      Find all 6 themed words hidden horizontally, vertically, or diagonally in the 8×8 grid.

      🔤 Daily Anagram Scramble

      Unscramble the 7 letters into the daily mystery word. Use the definition clue if you get stuck!

      🃏 Daily Memory Match

      Flip cards to find all 8 matching emoji pairs in as few moves as possible.

      🧩 Daily Sliding Tile Puzzle

      Slide numbered tiles into numerical order from 1 to 8 in the 3×3 grid.

      🎵 Daily Sequence Memory

      Watch and listen to the colored pads light up, then repeat the exact audio-visual sequence!

      📜 Daily Cryptogram

      Each letter in a famous inspirational quote has been swapped. Decipher the single-substitution cipher!
